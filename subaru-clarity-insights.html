SubaruSA — Clarity Insights

Ninety days of Microsoft Clarity data for subaru.co.za, read across session insights, click and tap heatmaps, JavaScript error logs, session recordings and Core Web Vitals field data. The audit identifies six faults where visitors interact with the site and nothing happens. The largest sits on /deal-assist/, which takes 44% of all site traffic: a single non-interactive container directly beneath the header absorbs 38.91% of every tap on the page — 4,534 taps across 33,467 mobile pageviews. Overall engagement on that page is 0.35 taps per pageview and 99.2% of visitors leave from the page they arrived on. Five further faults affect the showroom and pre-owned templates, and a group of globally-loaded scripts accounts for 84% of all JavaScript errors recorded on the site.

Section 01 · Method

How this was measured

i

Data sources and filtering

Microsoft Clarity project SubaruSA, filtered to the production domain only. Four other Subaru environments were found reporting into a separate Clarity project and have been excluded — see Open items. Heatmap filters use regex matching that includes query strings; filtering on exact URL excludes all campaign arrivals and understates affected pages by more than an order of magnitude. Extreme LCP outliers in the raw field data are backgrounded-tab artefacts and have been discarded. Gravity Forms entry exports were used to validate the behavioural findings, not to assess campaign performance.

Deal Assist fails by inertia. Visitors arrive, half never reach a second page, and the median session is under two minutes. Total engagement is 0.35 taps per pageview — most people touch nothing at all.

Forester fails by exhaustion. Five minutes, sixteen clicks, three pages, then 60% exit from the page they were on. The click distribution is the giveaway: 109 sessions recorded more than 50 clicks, 23 recorded more than 100, and one session recorded 267. That is not browsing.

Pre-owned visitors are the most engaged on the site. Six minutes, seven pages, twenty-four clicks, arriving mostly from Google and Bing. They are the closest visitors to a purchase decision, and the image gallery on the vehicle they are viewing does not advance.

The pattern across all three: the more intent a visitor has, the longer they will persist against an unresponsive interface before leaving.

Section 03 · Findings

Faults identified

1

Dead container absorbing 39% of taps — Deal Assist

Critical
/deal-assist/ takes 25,125 sessions, 44% of all traffic on the site and more than three times the homepage. It is 99% mobile — 33,467 mobile pageviews against 314 desktop. Of 11,652 recorded taps, 4,534 land on a single non-interactive container positioned directly beneath the header.

Visitors are tapping the container itself rather than any link inside it, which indicates there is nothing inside it to tap. It is the first element a mobile visitor sees, it reads as a button, and pressing it does nothing. A further ~670 taps land on static IMG elements and on headings being tapped as buttons.
Element
DIV.elementor-element.e-con-full.elementor-hidden-desktop.e-flex.e-con.e-parent:nth-of-type(2) > DIV.elementor-element.e-con-full.e-flex.e-con.e-child:nth-of-type(1)
4,534 taps · 38.91% of all taps on the page · mobile only
2

Used-vehicle photo gallery does not advance

Critical
On pre-owned detail pages, BUTTON.owl-next receives 3.1 clicks per pageview and roughly 80% of all clicks on the page. The previous arrow receives 90. A functioning gallery runs at about 3:1 next-to-previous; 31:1 indicates repeated pressing on a control that is not responding. Mobile shows the same pattern at 17.5:1.

This is an Owl Carousel, a different library from the Swiper used on the showroom pages, so it is a second carousel fault rather than the same one twice. For scale, the A.view-vehicle call to action on the same page receives 68 desktop clicks — the broken arrow outdraws it forty to one.

3

Elementor grade tabs throw on load

High
The Premium / Sport / Field / Touring / Comparison selector — the control a buyer uses to choose a trim — throws on every page load. Chrome reports cannot read properties of null (reading 'getAttribute'); Firefox gives the more useful tabTitleElement is null. Combined, these are 33.8% of all JavaScript errors recorded on the site, making this the largest single error source.

Worth checking while in there: the anchor #forester-field-New uses a capital N where every sibling uses -new, and it receives roughly a third of the clicks its siblings do.
~1,500 desktop and ~1,100 mobile interactions across the four showroom pages.
4

Broken scripts load site-wide, including on pages that do not use them

High
Deal Assist is a form page. It carries no vehicle cards, no grade tabs and no 360° viewer. Its error log contains all of them. This tells us the scripts are being enqueued globally from the theme or header rather than by the templates that need them — so this is one fix with sitewide benefit rather than several page-level repairs.

The duplicate-declaration error is the cheapest item in this report: a script parsed twice on the same page, typically once via functions.php and once pasted into an HTML widget or header code box. A half-initialised script is also the most probable cause of _self.view is not a function.

5

360° viewer never initialises

Medium
Three errors point at the same feature: isVrModeRequested is not defined, $threesixty.threesixty is not a function — the jQuery plugin failing to register at all — and heavy interaction with krpano panorama containers on the showroom pages. The Forester page carries four separate krpano instances, which is also a meaningful load cost on a template already running two seconds slower than the site's content pages.
3,028 desktop clicks across the four panorama instances, plus ~700 mobile taps on the colour swatches.
6

Popup is the most-tapped element on the Forester page

Medium
Counting the close button, its icon, the overlay and the popup body, roughly one tap in eight on the most important model page is someone dismissing an overlay — 1,201 mobile taps, 12.5% of all taps on the page.

Separately, failed to execute 'querySelector' fires because an #elementor-action:...popup:open href is being passed into a selector lookup, almost certainly by a smooth-scroll handler grabbing every a[href^="#"]. A second error, popup.getModal is not a function, points at the same feature.
Section 04 · Validation

Form data capture

1

Testing the diagnosis against form entries

Confirmed
Clarity measures behaviour, not outcomes. To check whether the dead container is genuinely the problem — rather than the form, the platform or the audience — we compared entry counts on the two lead forms over the same period. Both run on the same Gravity Forms install with the same dealer selector.
Book a test drive — sessions872
Book a test drive — genuine entries14
Deal Assist — mobile sessions~33,000
Deal Assist — genuine entries9
The test-drive page converts at a normal rate for an automotive enquiry form. Deal Assist receives roughly 38 times the traffic and produces fewer entries. That confirms the diagnosis: the form works — eleven submit taps produced twelve entries, so submission, validation and dealer capture all function correctly. The failure is upstream, at the point where visitors try to interact with the page.
Separately: the earliest Deal Assist entry is dated 1 September, with none before it, despite the page receiving traffic throughout. Worth establishing whether the form was absent, hidden or failing before that date.
2

Two fields have never captured data

Needs fixing
Vehicle Model is empty on all 93 entries across both forms, for the entire history of both. A second field, Select Your Model, populates correctly on every entry — so there are two model fields and one of them has never worked. Given the pattern of scripts failing site-wide, a hidden field populated by JavaScript that never runs is the likely explanation.

GA Client ID is empty on all 48 Deal Assist entries and on 26 of 45 test-drive entries. This hidden field ties a submission back to its GA4 session. Without it, form submissions cannot be joined to the sessions that produced them, which affects any reporting on where enquiries originate.
Roughly half of all entries on both forms are internal test submissions. Anyone counting entries to judge performance is counting test data alongside genuine enquiries.

2

Layout shift on pre-owned detail pages

Needs fixing
Site-wide CLS reads 0.002, which looks perfect, but the average conceals the problem. Individual pre-owned detail pages record 1.108, 0.865 and 0.728 against a 0.1 threshold — vehicle images rendering without width and height attributes, almost certainly arriving that way from the AutoTrader feed. Content moves under the reader's finger as photos load, on the pages where the most engaged visitors spend six minutes.

Section 07 · Unresolved

Open items & baseline

!

#toggleName — unverified

Needs a dead-click filter
What we know

#toggleName is the single most-interacted element on the showroom pages — 4,814 mobile taps and 2,775 desktop clicks across the four model pages, ahead of the tabs, the 360° viewer and everything else. Interaction volume that high has indicated a fault everywhere else on this site, but we have not yet confirmed it. Re-running the showroom heatmap with a dead-click filter applied will settle it.

!

Other Subaru environments in the analytics estate

Outside our scope
What we found

A separate Clarity project was receiving traffic from www.staging.subaru.co.za, newsubaru.clippingpointdev.co.za, subaru.clippingpointdev.co.za and subaru.pixelme.co.za. None of these are environments we build or maintain. The staging site scores 38 out of 100 and is publicly reachable; pixelme serves showroom and test-drive pages that duplicate the live site. Both are search-visibility exposures for subaru.co.za regardless of who owns them, and the client should confirm who runs them and whether they should remain live.

Related

www.subaru.co.za previously appeared as its own referrer on 27% of sessions — visitors crossing between Subaru domains mid-session, each crossing starting a new attributed session. The same distortion will be present in GA4. Two Clarity projects, "Subaru" and "SubaruSA", are also tracking the same site over the same period and reporting different numbers; one should be retired.
